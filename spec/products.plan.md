# SauceDemo Products and Sorting Test Plan

## Application Overview

Detailed functional test plan for the SauceDemo Products, sorting, product details, and cart add/remove modules after login with standard_user. Exploration confirmed the Swag Labs Products page, six product names, four sort option labels, the Sauce Labs Backpack details page, and add/remove behavior for the Backpack. Exact prices for the other five products, sorting result order, details navigation for every product, and cart add/remove behavior for every product were not fully verified during exploration; those items are explicitly marked for confirmation rather than assumed. No Playwright scripts are generated by this plan.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Products and Sorting

**Seed:** `Manual setup: start from a fresh SauceDemo session, log in as standard_user with password secret_sauce, and ensure the cart is empty.`

#### 1.1. TC-PROD-001 - Verify all six products are displayed

**File:** `Not generated - planning only`

**Steps:**
  1. Log in with standard_user and secret_sauce, then open the Products page.
    - expect: The Products page loads successfully.
    - expect: The product listing contains exactly six product cards.
  2. Record the visible product names from the listing.
    - expect: The six displayed products are Sauce Labs Backpack, Sauce Labs Bike Light, Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt, Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket, Sauce Labs Onesie, and Test.allTheThings() T-Shirt (Red).
    - expect: No expected product is missing and no unexpected product is present.
  3. Refresh the Products page and repeat the count and name check.
    - expect: The same six products remain displayed after refresh.
    - expect: The cart remains empty for this independent scenario.

#### 1.2. TC-PROD-002 - Verify product names and prices

**File:** `Not generated - planning only`

**Steps:**
  1. Starting from a fresh logged-in session, inspect each of the six product cards on the Products page.
    - expect: Each product card displays a product name and a currency-formatted price.
    - expect: The product names match the six expected names from TC-PROD-001.
  2. Record the exact displayed price for Sauce Labs Backpack.
    - expect: The Sauce Labs Backpack price is displayed as $29.99; this value was verified during exploration.
  3. Record and compare the displayed prices for Sauce Labs Bike Light, Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt, Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket, Sauce Labs Onesie, and Test.allTheThings() T-Shirt (Red).
    - expect: Each of the five products has a visible, correctly formatted price.
    - expect: The exact values for these five prices were not verified during exploration and must be confirmed from the current application; do not assume values from prior versions or external documentation.
  4. Open the Products page again after reviewing all cards.
    - expect: All six product names and their current prices remain visible and associated with the correct products.

#### 1.3. TC-SORT-001 - Verify all four product sorting options

**File:** `Not generated - planning only`

**Steps:**
  1. Open the Sort products control on a fresh Products page.
    - expect: A sorting control is available and initially shows Name (A to Z), which was observed during exploration.
  2. Inspect the available choices without selecting one.
    - expect: Exactly four choices are available: Name (A to Z), Name (Z to A), Price (low to high), and Price (high to low).
  3. Select Name (A to Z).
    - expect: The selection is accepted and the products are ordered alphabetically from A to Z. The resulting order was not behaviorally verified during exploration and must be confirmed.
  4. Select Name (Z to A).
    - expect: The selection is accepted and the products are ordered alphabetically from Z to A. The resulting order was not behaviorally verified during exploration and must be confirmed.
  5. Select Price (low to high).
    - expect: The selection is accepted and products are ordered from lowest displayed price to highest displayed price. The resulting order was not behaviorally verified during exploration and must be confirmed.
  6. Select Price (high to low).
    - expect: The selection is accepted and products are ordered from highest displayed price to lowest displayed price. The resulting order was not behaviorally verified during exploration and must be confirmed.
  7. Refresh the page after selecting a sort option.
    - expect: The application exhibits its defined persistence behavior for the selected sort; this persistence behavior was not verified during exploration and must be documented as observed rather than assumed.

### 2. Product Details and Cart

**Seed:** `Manual setup: start from a fresh SauceDemo session, log in as standard_user with password secret_sauce, open Products, and ensure the cart is empty before each test.`

#### 2.1. TC-DETAIL-001 - Verify navigation to product details

**File:** `Not generated - planning only`

**Steps:**
  1. From Products, select the product image or product-name/details control for each of the six products, one at a time.
    - expect: A product details page opens for the selected product.
    - expect: The details page identifies the same product name as the card that was selected.
    - expect: The page displays the product image, description, price, and an Add to cart control when the product is not already in the cart.
    - expect: A Back to products control is available.
  2. On the Sauce Labs Backpack details page, compare the displayed information with the Products card.
    - expect: The Backpack details page shows Sauce Labs Backpack, its description, and $29.99; this product-details flow was verified during exploration.
  3. Use Back to products and repeat for the remaining five products.
    - expect: The remaining five details flows must be confirmed during execution; navigation for those products was not fully verified during exploration and must not be assumed.

#### 2.2. TC-CART-001 - Verify adding products to the cart

**File:** `Not generated - planning only`

**Steps:**
  1. Start with an empty cart and open the Sauce Labs Backpack details page.
    - expect: The cart badge indicates zero items or is empty.
    - expect: The product control is labeled Add to cart.
  2. Click Add to cart.
    - expect: The product control changes to Remove.
    - expect: The cart badge updates to indicate one item.
    - expect: The product remains on the details page. These Backpack behaviors were verified during exploration.
  3. Return to Products and add each of the other five products once.
    - expect: Each selected product can be added and the cart count increases by one per product.
    - expect: Add behavior for the other five products was not fully verified during exploration and must be confirmed.
  4. Open the cart.
    - expect: The cart lists each product added, with the correct quantity and product information.
    - expect: The cart contents match the products selected.

#### 2.3. TC-CART-002 - Verify removing products from the cart and product details

**File:** `Not generated - planning only`

**Steps:**
  1. Add Sauce Labs Backpack and open its details page.
    - expect: The Backpack details control is labeled Remove and the cart badge shows one item; this state change was verified during exploration.
  2. Click Remove on the product details page.
    - expect: The control changes back to Add to cart.
    - expect: The cart badge returns to empty or zero items.
  3. Add Sauce Labs Backpack again, open the cart, and click Remove for the cart line item.
    - expect: The Backpack is removed from the cart.
    - expect: The cart badge returns to empty or zero items.
    - expect: The cart no longer lists the removed product. Cart-line removal was observed during exploration but should be re-confirmed in execution.
  4. Repeat add and remove once for each of the other five products.
    - expect: Each product can be removed from the cart and the count and cart contents update correctly.
    - expect: Removal behavior for the other five products was not fully verified during exploration and must be confirmed.
